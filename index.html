Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you need IELTS to apply? ▾
Most universities abroad require IELTS or PTE scores. However, some institutions offer conditional admission. Our counsellors will assess your profile and guide you on the best pathway, whether or not you have your scores yet.
How much IELTS score is required for a study visa? ▾
The required score varies by country and institution — typically 6.0 to 6.5 overall for most universities in Canada, Australia, and UK. We'll give you precise requirements based on your chosen course and country.
Can you offer package guidance? ▾
Yes! We offer comprehensive packages that include IELTS coaching, university shortlisting, SOP drafting, visa filing, and even post-visa services like accommodation and airport pickup.
Do you provide processing for Australia? ▾
Absolutely. Australia is one of our strongest destinations with direct partnerships with several Australian universities and a high visa approval rate for our students.
Are some examination boards suitable for study visa? ▾
Students with any board background — PSEB, CBSE, ICSE — can apply for a study visa. Our counsellors evaluate your academic history and recommend suitable universities and countries accordingly.
